EPOCH BREAKERS

A time-hopping ATB roguelite prototype

Draft your heroes, then explore three eras. Battles happen right where you touch an enemy. Gauges fill in real time. Let two or three heroes be ready at once to unleash Dual and Triple Techs, and discover them all across runs. Area techs depend on where enemies are standing, so time your blasts for when they bunch up.

Move: WASD / Arrows  ·  Battle: click buttons or 1-9, click to target, Esc / right-click cancel, Tab switch hero  ·  C: Codex
